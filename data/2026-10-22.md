### [1] [The moduli space of maps from hypersurfaces and its Goodwillie tower](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28809)
*Alexis Aumonier*

Main category: math.AT

TL;DR: 本文定义了一类"参数化映射模空间"（映射的定义域在丰沛线性系中变化的光滑超曲面），并构造扫描映射将其映到相应截面空间，借助Goodwillie微积分研究诱导同态在同调上的效应，同时计算了以任意retractive空间为基点的截面空间函子的Goodwillie塔。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 映射模空间的拓扑结构（如同调群）难以直接研究；已有扫描映射方法在具体模空间中缺乏统一、可推广的同调计算框架，作者希望给出一个适用于多类映射模空间的普遍论证。

Method: 1）定义光滑超曲面族（丰沛线性系中的变化）上连续映射构成的模空间；2）构造到适当截面空间的扫描映射；3）以Goodwillie微积分为核心工具，建立一般性论证来研究扫描映射在同调上的行为；4）计算以任意retractive空间为基点的截面空间函子的Goodwillie塔。

Result: 建立了扫描映射的同调分析框架（核心是一般化的Goodwillie微积分论证），并给出了截面空间函子在任意retractive空间基点下的Goodwillie塔的显式计算。

Conclusion: 该Goodwillie微积分式的论证具一般性，可推广到多种映射模空间，为研究其同调性质提供了统一工具。

Abstract: We define a moduli space of continuous maps from smooth hypersurfaces varying in an ample linear system. To study its topology, we construct a scanning map to an appropriate section space and investigate its effect on homology. Our main innovation is a general argument based on Goodwillie calculus and applicable to various moduli spaces of maps. Along the way, we compute the Goodwillie tower of the section space functor based at any retractive space.

</details>


### [2] [Pontryagin-Thom, $π_0$ of the motivic sphere, and principal quadratic divisors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29065)
*Kyle Ormsby*

Main category: math.AT

TL;DR: 该文在motivic情形下用Pontryagin-Thom方法计算motivic球谱的π₀，证明π₀(S_k)(k) ≅ GW(k)（无限完美域、char≠2），并将核ker(d)刻画为主二次除子群。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Segal用Pontryagin-Thom构造、横截性与marks计算了等变球谱的π₀为Burnside环。作者希望将同一论证搬到域k上的motivic情形，给出π₀(motivic球谱)的新计算，避免依赖Morel的计算。

Method: 将Burnside环替换为带单位装饰的有限étale k-概形的Grothendieck环A^u(Γ_k)，将marks替换为Dress映射d(L,u)=tr_{L/k}⟨u⟩到GW(k)，构造Pontryagin-Thom映射A^u(Γ_k)→π₀(S_k)(k)，证明其满且核为ker(d)；并用P¹上的互反律识别ker(d)为主二次除子群；横截性用初等的"re-topping"论证证明。

Result: 证明存在满射A^u(Γ_k)→π₀(S_k)(k)，核为ker(d)，从而对无限完美域、char≠2得到π₀(S_k)(k)≅GW(k)；并证明ker(d)等于有理函数的主二次除子群。

Conclusion: motivic球谱的π₀等于Γ_k的单位装饰Burnside环模主二次除子群，且可在不依赖Morel计算的情况下得到π₀(S_k)(k)≅GW(k)等结果。

Abstract: Segal computed $π_0$ of the equivariant sphere spectrum as the Burnside ring by a Pontryagin-Thom construction, transversality, and marks. We carry out the same argument in the motivic setting over a field $k$: the Burnside ring is replaced by the Grothendieck ring $A^u(Γ_k)$ of finite étale $k$-schemes decorated with a unit, the marks by the Dress map $d(L,u)=\mathrm{tr}_{L/k}\langle u\rangle$ into $\mathrm{GW}(k)$, and a Pontryagin-Thom map $A^u(Γ_k)\toπ_0(\mathbb{S}_k)(k)$ is shown to be surjective with kernel $\ker(d)$. This gives $π_0(\mathbb{S}_k)(k)\cong\mathrm{GW}(k)$ for infinite perfect fields of characteristic $\ne 2$ without Morel's computation. We then identify $\ker(d)$, by a reciprocity law on $\mathbb{P}^1$, as the group of principal quadratic divisors of rational functions, so that $π_0$ of the motivic sphere is the unit-decorated Burnside ring of $Γ_k$ modulo principal quadratic divisors. The transversality step is proved by an elementary "re-topping" argument.

</details>
