### [1] [Local $C^0$-semi-rigidity of meandering-hyperbolic actions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22216)
*Sungwoon Kim*

Main category: math.GT

TL;DR: 本文证明所有蜿蜒双曲作用在C^0拓扑下都是局部半刚性的，将Lipschitz C^0拓扑下的稳定性理论推广到完整的C^0拓扑，并由此统一恢复了词双曲群和半单李群中余紧格点的边界作用的C^0局部半刚性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 将Kapovich等人提出的蜿蜒双曲性概念下的稳定性理论从Lipschitz C^0拓扑推广到更一般的C^0拓扑，并统一解释已知的各类群作用的局部半刚性结果。

Method: 通过分析蜿蜒双曲作用的动力学性质，在C^0拓扑下建立局部半刚性的证明框架。

Result: 证明了每个蜿蜒双曲作用在C^0拓扑下都是局部半刚性的。

Conclusion: 该结果从统一的动力学原理出发，恢复了词双曲群边界作用和半单李群中余紧格点边界作用各自的C^0局部半刚性。

Abstract: Meandering hyperbolicity, introduced by Kapovich, Kim, and Lee, extends classical hyperbolicity beyond the setting of word-hyperbolic groups. In this paper, we prove that every meandering-hyperbolic action is locally semi-rigid in the $C^0$ topology. This extends the previously established stability theory in the Lipschitz $C^0$ topology to the full $C^0$ topology. Consequently, we recover the $C^0$-local semi-rigidity of both boundary actions of word-hyperbolic groups and cocompact lattices in semisimple Lie groups from a single dynamical principle.

### [2] [Non-existence of Cannon-Thurston maps for hierarchically hyperbolic groups](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22447)
*Funda Gültepe,Ravi Tomar*

Main category: math.GT

TL;DR: 本文证明了在层次双曲群的双曲正规子群中，Cannon-Thurston映射不存在（包括Morse边界和层次双曲边界），这一结论涵盖了亏格≥2曲面映射类群的大部分正规子群；作为推论，还证明了大多数自由-by-循环群子群中该映射也不存在。此外，证明了具有孤立平直的CAT(0)群的视觉边界与其作用空间的相对层次双曲边界同胚，从而恢复了已知的具有孤立平直的CAT(0)群的非存在性结果。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究Cannon-Thurston映射在层次双曲群正规子群中的存在性，尤其是映射类群和自由-by-循环群这类重要几何群论对象。

Method: 通过层次双曲群理论，结合Morse边界与相对层次双曲边界的技术，利用正规子群的结构性质进行证明；对于CAT(0)群，通过证明视觉边界与相对层次双曲边界同胚来重新推导已知结论。

Result: 层次双曲群的双曲正规子群不存在Cannon-Thurston映射；映射类群（亏格≥2）的大多数正规子群及大多数自由-by-循环群的子群均不满足该映射存在性；CAT(0)孤立平直群的视觉边界与相对层次双曲边界同胚。

Conclusion: Cannon-Thurston映射的存在性在层次双曲群框架下对正规子群有严格限制，论文给出的非存在性结果加深了对映射类群和自由-by-循环群几何结构的理解。

Abstract: We prove that Cannon--Thurston maps do not exist for hyperbolic normal subgroups of hierarchically hyperbolic groups, both for Morse and hierarchically hyperbolic boundaries. This result includes a large class of normal subgroups of the mapping class group of a surface of genus $g\geq 2$. As a corollary, we also prove a non-existence result for subgroups of most free by cyclic groups. Moreover, we prove that the visual boundary of a CAT(0) group with isolated flats is homeomorphic to the relatively hierarchically hyperbolic boundary of the space it acts on, thus recovering a previously known non-existence result for CAT(0) groups with isolated flats.

Main category: math.AT

TL;DR: 本文探讨了偏序集上的群复合体（simple complex of groups）及其与群K的嵌入关系，通过Bridson-Haefliger构造的K-等变同伦类型研究K的分类空间，并获得了上同调的有限性性质。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 利用嵌入群K的群复合体构造关联的K-偏序集，通过Bridson-Haefliger构造的K-等变同伦类型分析分类空间，并推导上同调性质。

Result: 获得了关于群K的分类空间的拓扑信息，以及上同调的有限性性质。

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统的完美离散Morse函数要求临界单元数量精确等于Betti数，但在多参数框架下需要从拓扑动力学角度考虑临界细胞自然聚集为连通分量的现象，因此需要更灵活的定义。

Abstract: We introduce the concept of component-perfect multidimensional discrete Morse (MDM) functions on finite regular CW complexes. Building upon the existing notion of strictly perfect MDM functions, we propose the more flexible definition of component-perfection.
  We also show how to restrict and extend MDM functions on finite regular CW complexes, laying the groundwork for more complex topological operations.

### [5] [On Grünbaum's problem for symmetric configurations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22032)
*Andrii Arman,Andriy Bondarenko,Andriy Prymak,Danylo Radchenko*

Main category: math.MG

TL;DR: 本文研究了覆盖直径为1的集合所需单位直径欧几里得球的最小数量$g_n$，针对坐标置换不变有限集合，证明该对称问题的指数增长率可由有限字母表平方误差率失真上确界$\alpha_0$精确刻画，并给出两点情形的显式下界$g_n\ge (1.160235457\ldots-o(1))^n$，改进了此前$(2/\sqrt3-o(1))^n$的最佳下界。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 确定覆盖直径为1的集合所需单位直径球的最小数量$g_n$是一个经典几何覆盖问题，现有下界不够紧，且对称结构下的精确增长率尚未被刻画。

Method: 利用坐标置换不变有限集合的对称性，将问题转化为有限字母表平方误差率失真问题，通过率失真理论的上确界$\alpha_0$刻画指数增长率。针对两点情形（布尔立方体子集）给出显式下界，并利用Fix的高斯刻画进行数值三点构造。

Result: 给出两点情形下界$g_n\ge (1.160235457\ldots-o(1))^n$，优于此前$(2/\sqrt3-o(1))^n$；三点构造得到指数基大于1.160497831；证明$\alpha_0$不能被任何有限支撑分布达到。

Conclusion: 对称覆盖问题的指数增长率可由率失真上确界$\alpha_0$精确刻画，该值非有限支撑分布可达，进一步改进需考虑无限支撑分布。

Abstract: Let $g_n$ be the largest number of Euclidean balls of diameter $1$ which may be needed to cover a set of diameter $1$ in $\mathbb{R}^n$. We study this problem for finite sets invariant under all coordinate permutations. We prove that the exponential growth rate in this symmetric problem can be characterized exactly as a finite-alphabet squared-error rate-distortion supremum $α_0$.
  Specialized to the two-point case, i.e., for subsets of Boolean cubes, this gives the explicit lower bound
  \[g_n\ge (1.160235457\ldots-o(1))^n,\] improving the previous best bound $(2/\sqrt3-o(1))^n$. Using Fix's Gaussian characterization of the rate-distortion problem, we give a numerical three-point construction with exponent base greater than $1.160497831$. Finally, we show that $α_0$ is not attained by any finitely supported distribution.

</details>


### [6] [Systolic inequalities for metric surfaces via filling minimality](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22290)
*Toni Ikonen,Denis Marti,Noa Vikman*

Main category: math.MG

TL;DR: 本文证明了亏格为一的环面和实射影平面上的最优 systolic 不等式，其最优常数与（可逆）Finsler 情形一致，推广了经典 Riemann 和 Finsler 曲面的结果。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 将经典 Riemann 和 Finsler 曲面上的 systolic 不等式推广到更一般的长度空间，并确定与 Finsler 情形相同的最优常数。

Method: 对于环面，通过分析万有覆盖的渐近体积增长，结合二维赋范平面面积极小性的强化结果；对于实射影平面，推广半球面积极小性结论。应用了近期度量圆盘的一致化定理和 Lytchak-Wenger 的度量空间面积极小盘理论。

Result: 建立了长度空间环面和实射影平面上的最优 systolic 不等式，最优常数与 Finsler 情形一致。

Conclusion: 将已知的 Riemann 和 Finsler 曲面上的最优 systolic 不等式成功推广到更一般的长度空间，为后续研究提供了新工具和视角。

Abstract: We prove optimal systolic inequalities for length spaces homeomorphic to a torus of genus one or a real projective plane. In both cases, the optimal constant coincides with the constant from the (reversible) Finsler setting. This generalizes the classical results for Riemannian and Finsler surfaces.
  The proof of the inequality for the torus relies on an analysis of the asymptotic volume growth of the universal cover, together with a strengthening of the previously known area minimality of two-dimensional normed planes. For the inequality of the real projective plane, we similarly extend a minimality result of hemispheres. These results build upon works of Burago-Ivanov and Ivanov, respectively. In their proofs we apply recent uniformization theorems for metric disks and the theory of area-minimizing disks in metric spaces due to Lytchak-Wenger.

</details>
