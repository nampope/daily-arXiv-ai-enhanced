### [1] [Braided Multisections and Akhmedov's Fake Quadrics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06949)
*Anar Akhmedov*

Main category: math.GT

TL;DR: 本文给出 Σ_g×S² 中循环多截面补集的手柄体分解，计算其整系数同调，并对 (3,3)、(2,4) 度对构造诱导指定一阶同调映射的曲面微分同胚，恢复整系数一阶同调群，且证明所得辛和为异步、辛极小的，并具有 S²×S² 的有理上同调环。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 论文关注先前构造的 Σ_g×S² 中循环多截面的补集，希望理解这些补集的拓扑结构（同调、torsion）及其与已有的映射构造（\cite{AY} 中规定的一阶同调映射）之间的关系，并研究相应辛和的几何性质。

Method: 对循环多截面补集建立手柄体分解，2-手柄的标架由乘积坐标诱导；通过手柄链复形计算整系数同调及 H₂ 的挠子群；对度对 (3,3) 与 (2,4) 显式构造实现指定一阶同调映射的曲面微分同胚；对 m=1 的对称和给出相对乘积附着的同时曲面图，识别乘积标架与曲面标架。

Result: 计算了补集的整系数同调，包括 H₂ 的挠子群；对 (3,3) 与 (2,4) 恢复整系数一阶同调群分别为 (Z/3)²⊕Z/(3m) 和 (Z/2)²⊕Z/(4m)（m≥1）；所得辛和为异步且辛极小，并具有 S²×S² 的有理上同调环。

Conclusion: 补集的手柄体分解结合乘积标架给出了其同调的完整计算，并给出满足 \cite{AY} 规定映射的显式微分同胚；在对称情形下将乘积标架与曲面标架等同，说明所得辛和保持了 S²×S² 的有理同调特征，同时为异步且辛极小。

Abstract: We give handlebody decompositions of the complements of the cyclic multisections in $Σ_g\times S^2$ constructed in~\cite{AB}. The 2-handle framings are induced by the product coordinates. We compute the integral homology of the complements from their handle chain complexes, including the torsion subgroup of $H_2$. For the degree pairs $(3,3)$ and $(2,4)$, we specify surface diffeomorphisms inducing the maps on first homology prescribed in~\cite{AY} and recover the integral first homology groups $(\Z/3)^2\oplus\Z/(3m)$ and $(\Z/2)^2\oplus\Z/(4m)$, respectively, for $m\geq1$. For the symmetric $m=1$ sum, we also give a simultaneous surface diagram of the relative product attachments and identify their product framings with the surface framings. The resulting symplectic sums are aspherical and symplectically minimal, and have the rational cohomology ring of $S^2\times S^2$.

### [2] [The residual map as a stratified fibration over the resonance arrangement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07499)
*Dawei Chen,Scott Mullane,Nick Salter,Guillaume Tahar*

Main category: math.GT

TL;DR: 证明了残差映射在共振重排诱导的每个开平坦上为局部平凡拓扑纤维化，并通过引入"残差对齐多尺度微分"紧化与平几何细胞分解给出代数几何与平几何两种证明。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究带指定零极点阶数的亚纯1-形式层$\mathcal{H}(\mu)$上残差映射的拓扑结构，理解共振条件下纤维化的分层行为，并建立其紧化与几何描述。

Method: 1) 代数几何：构造投影化层$\mathbb{P}\mathcal{H}(\mu)$的精细紧化RAMS($\mu$)，证明投影残差映射可延拓为该空间上的分层纤维化，从而给出映射的消解；2) 平几何：在亏格零情形下研究等残差纤维，发现其携带高维平移结构、度量完备化的WYSIWYG模解释与局部模型、多面体胞腔分解，其几何随周期坐标中残差构型线性变化；3) 推广至$k$-微分层。

Result: RAMS($\mu$) 给出投影残差映射的分层纤维化消解；亏格零时等残差纤维具规范高维平移结构、WYSIWYG边界模解释与显式局部模型；胞腔仅在共振条件下退化，由此给出分层纤维化的几何证明；部分等残差纤维同构于分支覆盖的Hurwitz空间，得到带指定分歧剖面的覆盖模空间间意外的同胚。

Conclusion: 残差映射在共振重排的开平坦上确为局部平凡纤维化；RAMS($\mu$)是合适的紧化工具且可推广至$k$-微分；平几何视角揭示等残差纤维的平移结构、胞腔分解与Hurwitz空间联系，加深了对层与覆盖模空间的理解。

Abstract: Given a stratum $\mathcal{H}(μ)$ of meromorphic one-forms on a Riemann surface with prescribed orders of zeros and poles, the residual map assigns to each differential the collection of its residues at the poles. In this paper, we prove that the residual map is a locally trivial topological fibration over each open flat of the stratification induced by the resonance arrangement.
  To give an algebro-geometric proof of this result, we introduce a refined compactification of the projectivized stratum $\mathbb{P}\mathcal{H}(μ)$, namely the moduli space of residue-aligned multi-scale differentials $\operatorname{RAMS}(μ)$. We prove that the projectivized residual map extends to $\operatorname{RAMS}(μ)$ as a stratified fibration over the projectivized resonance arrangement, thereby yielding a resolution of the projectivized residual map. This approach naturally extends to the analogue of the residual map for strata of $k$-differentials.
  Complementing the algebro-geometric approach, we also develop a flat-geometric point of view. In genus zero, each isoresidual fiber carries a canonical higher-dimensional analogue of a translation structure. These fibers admit a metric completion whose boundary has a modular interpretation in terms of the WYSIWYG compactification, for which we provide explicit local models. Isoresidual fibers admit a polyhedral cell decomposition whose geometry varies linearly with the underlying configuration of residues in period coordinates. Degenerations of these cells occur precisely when resonance conditions are satisfied, providing a geometric proof that the residual map is a stratified fibration. Finally, certain isoresidual fibers admit natural interpretations as Hurwitz spaces of branched covers, yielding unexpected homeomorphisms between moduli spaces of covers with prescribed ramification profiles.

### [3] [Using Transformers to Generate 3-Manifold Triangulations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07514)
*Daniel Bruwel,Ashvni Narayanan,Jonathan Spreer*

Main category: math.GT

TL;DR: 本文用脱水签名作为编码，训练GPT-2风格Transformer生成三维流形的三角剖分，并首次在该任务上观察到神经缩放规律（参数指数α≈0.49，最优约234 tokens/参数）。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探索生成式模型能否学习并生成拓扑上有效的三维流形三角剖分，同时研究该方法随计算量、参数量和数据量的缩放行为，以判断其可扩展性。

Method: 采用脱水签名（dehydration signature）作为三角剖分的序列编码，训练GPT-2式Transformer。主实验约3 exaFLOPs，从2亿参数/6000万个球面三角剖分到1100万参数/11亿个剖分。缩放研究用IsoFLOP曲线，在3-球面的15四面体剖分上覆盖6–3000 petaFLOPs计算量；另有若干小规模实验，如统计不同度边数的计数能力。

Result: 最优模型为10个block、宽度640，生成闭流形脱水签名的有效率为6.9%，其中82%为球面。缩放实验显示α=0.49（[0.48,0.50]统计误差，±0.02系统误差），最优约234 tokens/参数。Transformer能近乎完美地计数度为一的边，但随边度数增加计数能力逐渐下降。

Conclusion: Transformer可被训练用来自动生成多种拓扑类型的闭三维流形三角剖分，并表现出清晰的神经缩放规律；但生成有效率仍低（6.9%），且对高阶局部结构的计数能力有限，说明模型主要掌握简单拓扑特征。

Abstract: Using dehydration signatures as an encoding, we train GPT-2-style transformers to generate triangulations of 3-dimensional manifolds. Our main runs each use approximately 3 exaFLOPs, spanning a 200 million parameter model trained on 60 million sphere triangulations to an 11 million parameter model trained on 1.1 billion sphere triangulations. The optimal model has 10 blocks, width 640 and generates dehydration signatures of closed manifolds of various topological types at a rate of 6.9%, 82% of which are spheres. To investigate how our method scales, we use the technique of IsoFLOP curves on 15-tetrahedron triangulations of the 3-sphere, over a compute range of 6 to 3000 petaFLOPs, and observe neural scaling behaviour. We extract a parameter scaling exponent of $α= 0.49$ ($[0.48, 0.50]$ stat, $\pm 0.02$ syst), and an optimal ratio of 234 tokens per parameter for training. In addition, we present a number of smaller experiments, including evidence that transformers are able to learn to count degree-one edges near-perfectly, but get progressively worse when counting edges with higher degrees.

### [4] [Counting multigeodesics and the Selberg-Delange method](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08165)
*Aapo Karvonen*

Main category: math.GT

TL;DR: 用 Selberg-Delange 方法证明紧双曲曲面上 m-多重测地线计数函数 P^m(X,x) 的大 x 渐近公式，并证明 ω_1 在长度 ≤ x 的多重测地线中服从参数 log x 的 Poisson 分布。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 整数中恰含 m 个素因子的计数（Selberg-Delange 方法）已有成熟理论，作者希望把这一算术计数框架搬到双曲曲面上，研究闭测地线的"多重分解"结构，并把 m 固定与 m/log x 有界两种情形区分开。

Method: 构建 ω_1 的生成函数，将其与 Selberg zeta 函数相联系；再套用 Selberg-Delange 方法对生成函数做解析处理，得到 P^m(X,x) 的渐近展开。Poisson 分布结论通过对该生成函数在 x 大时的分析得到。

Result: 对固定 m，x→∞ 时 P^m(X,x) 的渐近与曲面 X 无关；当 m/log x 远离 0 时，渐近中出现由本原闭测地线长度构成的无穷 Euler 乘积。ω_1 在多测地线集合中的分布逼近参数为 log x 的 Poisson 随机变量。

Conclusion: 多重测地线计数具有与整数素因子计数平行的结构：小 m 情形呈"普适"行为，大 m 情形则由 Euler 乘积主导；ω_1 呈 Poisson 型极限分布，说明多重测地线的因子结构在统计上类似随机整数的素因子。

Abstract: We study the counting function $\mathcal{P}^m(X,x)$ for $m$-multigeodesics of length at most $x$ on a compact hyperbolic surface $X$ where $m$ is a positive integer. Building on the Selberg-Delange method, originally developed for counting integers with exactly $m$ prime factors in the interval $[1,x]$, we prove a large $x$ asymptotic for $\mathcal{P}^m(X,x)$. We show that for fixed $m$ as $x\to\infty$ the asymptotic is independent of the surface, whereas for $\frac{m}{\log x}$ bounded away from zero, the asymptotic depends on an infinite Euler product over the lengths of primitive closed geodesics. We also study the distribution of the function $ω_1$, defined on multigeodesics $Γ$ as $ω_1(Γ)=k-1$ for $Γ=(γ_1,...,γ_k)$ a $k$-multigeodesic. We show that as $x\to\infty$, the distribution of $ω_1$ in the set of multigeodesics of length $\leq x$ is close to that of a Poisson random variable with parameter $\log x$. Both of these theorems rely on studying the generating function of the random variable $ω_1$ which we relate to the Selberg zeta function.

</details>
