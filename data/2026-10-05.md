Result: 证明了Bishop–Gromov不等式可作为度量曲面曲率下界的刻画条件。

Conclusion: 给出了度量曲面曲率下界的一个Bishop–Gromov型等价刻画，将比较几何推广到非光滑度量曲面情形。

Abstract: We characterize lower curvature bounds on metric surfaces by the Bishop--Gromov inequality for metric balls.
