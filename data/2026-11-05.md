### [1] [Non-Pseudo-Arithmetic Hyperbolic 4-Manifolds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05795)
*Alexander Kolpakov*

Main category: math.GT

TL;DR: 给出三个四维有限体积双曲Coxeter格（Ma–Zheng多面体 P_{11,6}, P_{11,7}, P_{11,8} 的反射群），证明其伴随迹域非全实，从而它们不是伪算术的。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 判定这些四维双曲Coxeter格是否为（伪）算术格；需要检验伴随迹域的全实性这一Emery–Mila障碍条件。

Method: 从加倍单位法向Gram矩阵 A=2G 的两个循环乘积中提取元素 s=√5 和 u，满足二次方程 (s-1)u²+2(s-3)u+s-7=0；经嵌入 s↦-√5 后判别式为 8(1-√5)<0，说明循环乘积域含复嵌入。借助 Vinberg 的"循环乘积域=伴随迹域"识别与 Emery–Mila 全实性障碍得出结论；并用精确计算与 Lean 4 形式化验证。

Result: 循环乘积域存在复素位，故伴随迹域非全实；三个格均非伪算术。由此得到具有相同障碍的可定向非紧有限体积双曲4-流形。精确计算核验了普查数据，Lean 4 验证了根式恒等式与数域障碍及相应推论（含显式几何与文献假设）。

Conclusion: Ma–Zheng 三个四维Coxeter格及其子群给出的双曲4-流形非伪算术，提供了新的非算术/非伪算术实例，并附带计算与形式化证书。

Abstract: We give three explicit finite-volume hyperbolic Coxeter lattices in dimension four whose adjoint trace fields are not totally real. They are the reflection groups of the Ma--Zheng polytopes $P_{11,6}$, $P_{11,7}$, $P_{11,8}$. The obstruction is contained in two cyclic products of the doubled unit-normal Gram matrix $A=2G$. Those products recover elements $s=\sqrt5$ and $u$ satisfying $$ (s-1)u^2+2(s-3)u+s-7=0. $$
  After the embedding $s\mapsto-\sqrt5$, this quadratic has discriminant $8(1-\sqrt5)<0$. The cyclic-product field therefore has a complex place. Vinberg's identification of that field with the adjoint trace field and the Emery--Mila total-reality obstruction imply that the three lattices are not pseudo-arithmetic. Torsion-free orientation-preserving finite-index subgroups yield orientable noncompact finite-volume hyperbolic $4$-manifolds with the same obstruction.
  Exact computations check the census data and provide independent field and finite-volume certificates. A pinned Lean~4 development verifies the radical identity and the number-field obstruction. It also checks the lattice and manifold implications conditional on explicit geometric and literature hypotheses.

</details>


### [2] [On the (in)finiteness of fixed characters in genus $3$](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06357)
*Yixuan Fu*

Main category: math.GT

TL;DR: 在亏格为3的情形下构造了Putman–Wieland猜想的一个反例，并讨论其若干推论。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Putman–Wieland猜想是映射类群表示论与同调稳定性的相关问题，此前缺乏反例，作者旨在检验该猜想的正确性。

Method: 通过显式构造亏格3曲面情形下的反例（推测涉及映射类群在特定上同调/同调表示上的作用计算）。

Result: 给出亏格3时Putman–Wieland猜想成立失败的具体反例。

Conclusion: 该猜想在一般情形下不成立，需修正或限定适用范围，并由此引出相关推论。

Abstract: We construct a counterexample to the Putman--Wieland conjecture in genus $3$ and discuss some of its consequences.

</details>


### [3] [3-manifold decision problems and Riemann hypotheses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06705)
*John A. Baldwin,Steven Sivek*

Main category: math.GT

TL;DR: 本文说明如何利用"准黎曼假设"（quasi-Riemann hypothesis）的最新进展，将3维流形拓扑中若干决策问题（如3-球面识别）在coNP中的结果从"依赖广义黎曼假设"升级为无条件成立，首次给出3-球面识别属于coNP的无条件证明。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 自Kuperberg关于unknot recognition的工作以来，3维流形拓扑中的多个基本决策问题被证明在广义黎曼假设（GRH）下属于coNP，但这些结果都依赖于未证明的假设，缺乏无条件性。作者希望去除这一前提。

Method: 借助近期围绕准黎曼假设（quasi-Riemann hypothesis）的研究进展，把原证明中对GRH的依赖替换为(在准黎曼假设下)无条件可得的数论/算术结论，从而重构这些决策问题的coNP成员性证明。这是一篇说明性(注记)文章。

Result: 得到若干3维流形决策问题的无条件coNP成员性证明，其中包括首个3-球面识别(3-sphere recognition)属于coNP的无条件证明。

Conclusion: 准黎曼假设的最新进展足以消除此前结果对广义黎曼假设的依赖，使3维流形拓扑中的一系列基本决策问题获得无条件复杂度结论；这是一篇总结性注记。

Abstract: Beginning with Kuperberg's work on unknot recognition, several fundamental decision problems in 3-manifold topology have been shown to be in coNP assuming the generalized Riemann hypothesis. In this note, we explain how recent developments around the quasi-Riemann hypothesis yield unconditional versions of these results, leading for example to the first unconditional proof that 3-sphere recognition is in coNP.

</details>


### [4] [Chromatic Spherical and Renormalized Reshetikhin-Turaev Invariants for Drinfeld Centers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06708)
*Nicolas Bridges,Shawn Cui,Nathan Geer*

Main category: math.GT

TL;DR: 本文构造了闭连通可定向三维流形与着色带状图配对(M,Γ)的不变量，推广了染色球面不变量与重整化Reshetikhin-Turaev不变量。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 推广三维流形不变量到含framed ribbon graph的配对情形，并建立染色球面不变量与重整化Reshetikhin-Turaev不变量之间的关系，推广Reina关于幺模Hopf代数的近期结果。

Method: 使用Heegaard-Graph图表示配对(M,Γ)，利用幺模染色范畴(unimodular chromatic category)的数据及其Drinfeld中心的对象构造不变量；将嵌入framed link关联到ribbon graph；通过从C诱导出中心上的染色结构建立联系，并给出由Heegaard-Link图导出的M(L)的Heegaard图。

Result: 当L为空链时恢复染色球面不变量；当M为三维球面时恢复Drinfeld中心的重整化RT不变量；主定理表明当图是以Drinfeld中心生成元着色的链L时，可恢复手术流形M(L)的染色球面不变量。

Conclusion: 为幺模染色范畴下染色球面不变量与重整化RT不变量的关系提供了证明，推广了Reina关于幺模Hopf代数的结果。

Abstract: We give invariants of pairs $(M,Γ)$ consisting of a closed connected oriented three-manifold and an oriented framed ribbon graph embedded in $M$ colored by a the Drinfeld center of a unimodular chromatic category $\mathcal C$. This invariant generalizes the chromatic spherical and renormalized Reshetikhin-Turaev invariants of three-manifolds. We use Heegaard-Graph diagrams which represent the pair $(M,Γ)$ and use the data of a unimodular chromatic category and objects of the Drinfeld center to construct the invariant. We define a ribbon graph associated to embedded framed links to obtain an invariant of pairs $(M,L)$. We show that if $L$ is the empty link, then the invariant recovers the chromatic spherical invariant, and if $M$ is the three-sphere, then the invariant recovers the renormalized Reshetikhin-Turaev invariant from the Drinfeld center. The relationship is proven by recovering a chromatic structure for the center induced by that from $\mathcal C$. The main theorem of the paper demonstrates that if the graph is a link $L$ colored by a generator in the Drinfeld center, then we recover the chromatic spherical invariant of the surgery manifold $M(L)$, contributing to a proof of the relationship between the chromatic spherical and renormalized Reshetikhin-Turaev invariants for unimodular chromatic categories, generalizing a recent result for unimodular Hopf algebras by Reina. To this end, we give a Heegaard diagram for $M(L)$ coming from the Heegaard-Link diagram representing the pair $(M,L)$.

</details>
